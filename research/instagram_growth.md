# @kacsifir: Instagram Reels growth research (as of 2026-10-21)

Scope: how to grow a new Turkish-language, faceless, code-animated "numbers / scale / myth" Reels account
posting 3 Reels/day through Buffer. Evidence levels used below:

- **[OFFICIAL]**: Instagram, Meta, Mosseri or @creators, or a direct report of their words.
- **[DATA]**: third-party dataset with a stated sample.
- **[BLOG]**: marketing blog or vendor claim with no primary source. Treat as a hypothesis.
- **[ANECDOTE]**: self-reported by a single creator or vendor.

---

## 0. Bottom line, and how realistic 5,000 followers in week 1 is

- **5,000 followers in the first 7 days is not a realistic baseline.** It happens only if one Reel breaks
  out to millions of non-follower views. No dataset measures week-1 growth for new accounts. Every
  benchmark found is either for established brand pages or is self-reported (section 5).
- The evidence supports a realistic range of **0–500 followers in week 1 and roughly 300–3,000 in month 1**
  without a breakout. A single viral Reel (1–5M views) can add 2,000–10,000 in a few days.
- Follow conversion is the bottleneck. In one agency case, a 250K-view Reel produced 80 follows, which
  is 0.03% **[ANECDOTE]**. Even at an optimistic 0.1–0.3% follow rate, 5,000 follows needs about
  1.7–5M views in week 1.
- What moves the needle is **sends per reach (DM shares) and watch time on non-follower reach**, plus a
  profile that turns viewers into followers (a clear series promise in the name, bio and grid). Posting
  volume helps only while quality holds.

## 1. How Reels are ranked for non-followers (2025–2026)

### 1.1 Signals

| Signal | Evidence | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Watch time, likes, sends** are "the top three signals". Likes weigh a bit more for followers ("connected"); **sends weigh a bit more for non-followers ("unconnected")**. Mosseri tells creators to track average watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach. | [OFFICIAL] Mosseri video, 22 Jan 2025 | https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/ |
| Watch time counts both the **% watched and the absolute seconds watched**. | [OFFICIAL, reported] Mosseri, Feb 2025 | https://www.highstyle.ai/insights/instagram-reels-algorithm-2026 (secondary) |
| **Staged distribution.** A new Reel goes first to a small non-follower audience likely to enjoy it. If it performs, it expands in waves. Signals named: watch time, retention, shares, likes, comments, plus interest matching. Distribution is based on engagement, **not follower count**. | [OFFICIAL] @creators, 26 Feb 2025 | https://creators.instagram.com/blog/helping-creators-of-all-sizes-break-through |
| Explore weights how **fast and how many** people like, comment, share and save. | [OFFICIAL] Instagram Ranking Explained, 31 May 2023 (older, still the only official signal list) | https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained |
| Claims that "sends are worth 3–5× likes" | [BLOG], no primary source. Ignore the number and keep the direction. | https://socialday.live/features/instagrams-dm-share-signal-now-drives-3-to-5-times-more-reach-than-likes |
| Industry trend: Reel **shares +67% YoY** and average watch time per Reel **+117% vs 2025** | [DATA] Metricool, 24.4M posts / 375K accounts, Jun 2026 | https://metricool.com/instagram-trends/ |

### 1.2 Skip rate and the first 3 seconds

- Instagram replaced "view rate" with a **Skip rate** metric: the share of plays that scroll away within
  the first 3 seconds. It sits next to a **Retention chart** (around Aug 2025) **[OFFICIAL, API]**. See
  https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/reference/instagram-media/insights/ and
  https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/instagram-retention-chart-skip-rate-new-performance-metrics-reels-9730992
- Instagram's own tip, in its Turkish creator page: **grab attention in the first 3 seconds and keep it to the end** **[OFFICIAL]**.
  https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators
- Benchmark: brand accounts with **1–5K followers average a 65.5% skip rate**, and 100K–1M accounts 60.5%
  **[DATA]** (Socialinsider, 9.77M Reels, H1 2026). Beating about 60% puts you ahead of most accounts.
  https://socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-reels-statistics/

### 1.3 Length

- Reels must be **≤ 3 minutes to be recommended** to non-followers. The limit was 90 s before January 2025
  **[OFFICIAL]**. https://creators.instagram.com/blog/the-latest-with-instagram ;
  https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-reels-tips-3-minutes-unfiltered-nyc/741722/
- Reach rate by length: **30–60 s = 5.6% (best)**, <30 s = 5.2%, 60–90 s = 5.3%, 90–120 s = 4.3%, >120 s = 3.5%
  **[DATA]** (Socialinsider H1 2026, brand accounts).
- For @kacsifir, aim for **20–45 s**: one number, one reveal, a short payoff, then a loop back to the start.

### 1.4 Originality (the April 2026 update) and what gets demoted

- **30 Apr 2026:** accounts that mostly repost other people's photos or carousels became **ineligible for
  recommendations** (Feed suggestions, Explore). This rule already applied to Reels. Followers still see
  the posts. Original means content "wholly created" or reflecting a "unique perspective", or
  substantially edited (commentary, unique text, creative edits, voice-over). **Watermarks, speed changes
  and credited screenshots do not count as transformation.** **[OFFICIAL, via TechCrunch]**
  https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/instagram-restricts-reach-of-content-aggregators-in-new-crackdown/ ;
  https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/04/30/instagram-removes-algorithm-recommendations-repost-content-aggregator/
- Reported recovery rule: an account regains eligibility when **most of its posts in the last 30 days** are
  original **[BLOG, consistent across outlets, not verified on Instagram's page]**.
  https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/instagram-original-content-policy-11801189
- Instagram shows fewer **low-resolution or watermarked videos, muted videos, videos with borders, "majority-text"
  videos and videos already posted on Instagram** **[OFFICIAL]** (Ranking Explained, 2023). The Turkish
  creators page adds: use **high-resolution, borderless 9:16** video, with **text that does not cover most of
  the screen**, and do not re-upload Reels already on Instagram.
  → **Key risk for @kacsifir:** a numbers channel can drift into "slides of text". Keep the frame
  image-led (drawing, map, scale objects). Text should be a short label, not a paragraph. Never post a
  muted Reel.
- **AI-generated people:** since **31 Aug 2026** profiles featuring an AI-generated person must carry the
  "AI-generated profile" label, or they lose recommendation eligibility. The label is not required just
  because AI tools were used **[OFFICIAL, via news coverage]**. Code-drawn diagrams with no realistic
  human are outside this rule. https://www.outlookbusiness.com/corporate/instagrams-new-ai-rule-undisclosed-ai-generated-profiles-could-lose-reach ;
  https://gulfnews.com/technology/media/instagram-cracks-down-on-ai-influencers-pretending-to-be-real-people-1.500659801
- Mosseri's year-end 2025 essay: as synthetic content floods feeds, Instagram will lean toward
  **authenticity, original voice and transformation** **[OFFICIAL, reported]**.
  https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/01/06/adam-mosseri-instagram-warning-generative-ai-deepfakes/
- **Engagement bait** ("comment YES", "tag 3 friends"): Meta has demoted it on Facebook since 2017 and
  lists it as a demotion category in its Transparency Center. I found **no Instagram-specific 2025–26 rule
  text**. Treat it as a risk and phrase prompts as genuine questions **[OFFICIAL for FB; IG unverified]**.
  https://transparency.meta.com/features/approach-to-ranking/types-of-content-we-demote/

### 1.5 Recommendation eligibility (check this on day 1)

- Professional accounts can check **Settings → Account Status → "Can your content be recommended?"**. One
  violation in the profile (photo, bio) makes **all** content ineligible **[OFFICIAL]**.
  https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators
- Content about violence, tobacco/vape or sexual suggestiveness is not recommended. Watch the
  myth-busting topics: drug, alcohol and weapon statistics can trip this.

### 1.6 Trial Reels

- Trial Reels are shown **only to non-followers**. They can auto-share to followers if they perform
  ("SS_PERFORMANCE"), or you share them manually. Mosseri: **"Trials are not going to hurt your account."**
  **[OFFICIAL]** https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels ;
  https://www.kontentino.com/q-and-a/common-questions-about-instagram-threads-facebook (Aug 2025)
- Eligibility: a public professional account. Follower thresholds of **200 (professional) or 1,000
  (personal)** are cited from the Help Center by blogs, but not verified directly. Access is staggered
  **[BLOG]**. https://creatortribune.com/instagram-trial-reels/
- **Buffer supports Trial Reels** (Off / Auto / Manual), but not combined with collaborators **[OFFICIAL Buffer docs]**.
  The Graph API supports it via `trial_params` **[OFFICIAL Meta changelog]**.
  https://support.buffer.com/article/657-scheduling-instagram-posts-and-reels ;
  https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/changelog
- With 3 posts/day, once eligible, use **1 of the 3 daily slots as a Trial Reel** to test hooks without
  diluting the grid.

### 1.7 Posting frequency

- Mosseri, across several Q&As: **more posts generally means more reach and followers**, as long as quality and
  sustainability hold. There is no golden number **[OFFICIAL, reported]**.
  https://www.socialpilot.co/instagram-marketing/how-often-to-post-on-instagram ;
  https://routenote.com/blog/how-often-you-should-post-on-instagram/
- Metricool (H1 2026): small accounts (2K–10K) peak in interactions at **7–14 posts/week**, mid-size
  (10K–100K) at 14–30/week **[DATA]**. At 21/week, @kacsifir is above the small-account sweet spot. That
  is fine only if every Reel clears a quality bar. If skip rate or watch time drops, cut to 2/day.

### 1.8 Cover and first frame

- Buffer auto-publish **uses a video frame as the cover** (default 0 s). **Custom cover uploads are not
  supported** **[OFFICIAL Buffer docs]**. Frame 0 is therefore the thumbnail on the grid and in search.
  Design it as a poster: the big number plus a 2–4 word Turkish keyword line, inside the 4:5/1:1 grid crop.

### 1.9 Audio (correction to the brief's assumption)

- **Since 7 Oct 2026 Buffer can attach Instagram library music** to a Reel and still auto-publish, when the
  channel is connected through Facebook **[OFFICIAL Buffer changelog]**. So "no trending audio" is no longer
  a hard constraint. https://buffer.com/changelog/music-collaborators-and-more-for-instagram
- Caveat: **Business** accounts are limited to the commercial / Meta Sound Collection library. One source says
  **Creator** accounts keep the full library **[BLOG, unverified]**. Use a **Creator** account.
  https://www.soundstripe.com/blogs/instagram-music-library
- Original audio is still fine, and Instagram links your original audio to you so others can reuse it
  **[OFFICIAL]**. Muted Reels are demoted, so audio must always be present.

## 2. Instagram SEO and hashtags

### 2.1 Hashtag cap: **5 per post or Reel** (confirmed)

- @creators, **18 Dec 2025**: "Instagram will gradually update the number of hashtags that you can include in
  a caption for a reel or post to five." It adds that using **fewer (up to 5), more targeted hashtags rather
  than many generic ones** can improve performance **[OFFICIAL]**.
  https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/instagram-hashtags-five-per-post-10923075
- **Buffer currently enforces 5 in the caption and up to 30 in the first comment** **[OFFICIAL Buffer docs]**.
  Whether comment hashtags help, or count against the cap, is unclear. Recommendation: **max 5, all in the
  caption** (Instagram says hashtags belong in captions, not comments).
- Hashtag following was removed in December 2024. Metricool finds posts **with** hashtags got 31% fewer
  views than posts without. This is a correlation, likely driven by spammy accounts **[DATA]**.
  https://metricool.com/instagram-trends/
- Mosseri, repeatedly: hashtags help Instagram **understand** a post, but are **not a distribution lever**
  **[OFFICIAL, reported]**. https://www.phable.io/phable-labs/instagram-hashtags-keywords-discovery
- **Rule: 3–5 specific tags.** Use 1 niche category tag, 1–2 topic tags and 1 brand tag. Never use
  #reels, #keşfet, #explore or #viral; Instagram specifically calls generic tags like these out.

### 2.2 Keywords (search)

- Instagram's own tips: **use a relevant username / profile name, put keywords and topic in the bio, put
  keywords and hashtags in the caption (not in comments)** so posts appear in search **[OFFICIAL]**.
  https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators
- Instagram reads on-screen text and audio for search. This is widely claimed but **[BLOG, not
  confirmed]**. Alt text is used, but its weight is unconfirmed **[BLOG]**.
  https://metricool.com/instagram-seo/
- Since **10 Jul 2025**, public posts from professional accounts (18+) are **indexed by Google and Bing** by
  default **[OFFICIAL, via trade press]**. Turkish captions are also Google SEO.
  https://metricool.com/instagram-indexing-on-google/
- Users can now **edit their Reels topics ("Your Algorithm")**. It rolled out in the US in December 2025, with
  global English planned, so it does not apply to Turkish yet. Clear, consistent topic signals matter.
  https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/411322/

### 2.3 Practical SEO setup for @kacsifir

- **Name field** (searchable, separate from @handle): e.g. `Kaç Sıfır? | İlginç Bilgiler & Sayılar`.
  Include the keywords Turks actually search: *ilginç bilgiler, bilgi, bilim, sayılar*.
- **Bio line 1** states the promise in keywords. Example: "Akıl almaz sayılar, ölçek karşılaştırmaları ve
  efsanelerin gerçeği. Her gün 3 yeni bilgi."
- **Caption line 1** is a natural Turkish sentence with the topic keyword ("Dünyadaki tüm karıncalar
  Türkiye'nin %64'ünü kaplar."), then 1–3 lines of context and a source line, then a question, then
  ≤ 5 hashtags.
- **On-screen keyword:** put the topic noun (e.g. "KARINCA") as a label in the first second.
- Check Turkish casing: İ/ı (e.g. "İLGİNÇ", not "ILGINÇ").

## 4. Tactics reported by faceless and educational creators (mostly [BLOG]/[ANECDOTE])

Reddit could not be fetched directly (blocked), and site-restricted searches returned no threads. The tactics
below come from creator blogs and case studies and are labeled accordingly.

1. **Hook = the payoff first, as an image.** State the shocking number or show the impossible comparison
   in frame 0–1 s, then explain. Open loops ("…ama asıl şaşırtıcı olan sonda") hold viewers to the end
   **[BLOG]**. https://klap.app/blog/how-long-should-reels-be , https://tlinky.com/3-second-hook/
2. **Hook text plus the answer in the caption:** creators report people expand the caption to read the
   answer, which adds dwell time **[ANECDOTE]**. https://bellafoxwell.substack.com/p/5-juicy-instagram-lessons
3. **Design for sends.** Mosseri: make things people want to send to someone they care about; don't game
   it **[OFFICIAL]**. For @kacsifir: "Bunu bilmeyen birine gönder", comparisons with Turkish anchors
   (Türkiye, İstanbul, Boğaz, lira), and "challenge your friend" numbers ("Arkadaşına sor: kaç sıfır?").
   https://influencermarketinghub.com/instagram-sends-per-reach-playbook/
4. **Series format.** A recurring name ("Kaç Sıfır? #12") and the same opening grammar build follow
   intent: viewers follow to get the next one. The profile grid should look like a numbered collection.
5. **Genuine comment questions**, not bait. Ask for a guess ("Sence kaç sıfır? Tahminini yaz"). The guess
   format is native to the brand name.
6. **Pinned comment** with the source or citation plus a follow-up teaser. This also covers credibility
   for myth-busting.
7. **Collab posts.** Creator-initiated collabs are associated with higher organic performance, strongest on
   Reels **[DATA, correlational, Emplifi H1 2026]**. Buffer supports up to 3 collaborators but not with Trial
   Reels. Target Turkish science, history and geography creators for co-authored episodes.
   https://emplifi.io/resources/instagram-collaborations-boost-organic-engagement/
8. **Reuse winners, but transform them.** Identical re-uploads are demoted ("already posted on Instagram"
   **[OFFICIAL]**). Re-cut a winner with a new hook, new comparison or new aspect instead. Or share it to
   Stories, or use the native Repost button.
9. **Cross-post** clean, watermark-free exports to YouTube Shorts and TikTok. Mosseri also recommends
   cross-posting to Facebook and Threads for extra reach **[OFFICIAL]**.
10. **Fix the profile before the views come.** A 250K-view Reel gave 80 follows until the bio and CTA were
    fixed **[ANECDOTE]**. https://www.upgrow.com/blog/how-reels-go-viral-case-study-breakdown

## 6. Top 15 rules, ranked by expected impact

1. **Optimize for sends per reach.** Make every episode something a Turk would DM to a friend: Turkish
   anchors, "can you believe" numbers, a guess challenge. Track sends/reach per Reel.
   https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/
2. **Win the first 3 seconds.** Frame 0 shows the number or impossible comparison as an image, motion
   starts by 0.5 s, and there is no intro. Target a skip rate below 60% (the small-account average is 65.5%).
   https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators ·
   https://socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-reels-statistics/
3. **Maximize watch time with 20–45 s Reels and a seamless loop.** Reach peaks at 30–60 s and drops past 90 s.
   https://socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-reels-statistics/
4. **Stay 100% original and image-led.** No majority-text frames, no borders, no watermarks, never muted,
   never re-upload. https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained ·
   https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/instagram-restricts-reach-of-content-aggregators-in-new-crackdown/
5. **Verify recommendation eligibility on day 1**, then weekly (Account Status), and keep the profile clean.
   https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators
6. **Make each Reel standalone for non-followers.** Assume zero context; the payoff lands inside the Reel.
   https://creators.instagram.com/blog/helping-creators-of-all-sizes-break-through
7. **Use Trial Reels as soon as the account is eligible** (Buffer: Trial = Auto) for 1 of the 3 daily slots, to
   test hooks. https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels ·
   https://support.buffer.com/article/657-scheduling-instagram-posts-and-reels
8. **Turn viewers into followers.** Use a numbered series identity, a name field and bio that promise "her
   gün 3 akıl almaz sayı", and a grid that reads as a collection.
   https://www.upgrow.com/blog/how-reels-go-viral-case-study-breakdown
9. **Post volume: keep 3/day only while quality holds.** More posts means more reach (Mosseri). Small accounts
   peak at 7–14/week (Metricool). Cut to 2/day if retention falls.
   https://www.socialpilot.co/instagram-marketing/how-often-to-post-on-instagram · https://metricool.com/instagram-trends/
10. **Keyword SEO.** Put Turkish keywords in the name field, bio line 1, caption line 1 and an on-screen
    topic label. https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators
11. **Use at most 5 hashtags, specific and in the caption.** Use #ilginçbilgiler + #bilgi/#bilim + topic +
    #kaçsıfır, and never #keşfet/#reels. https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/instagram-hashtags-five-per-post-10923075
12. **Frame 0 is the cover** (Buffer can't upload custom covers). Design it as a poster that is legible in the
    4:5 grid crop. https://support.buffer.com/article/657-scheduling-instagram-posts-and-reels
13. **Ask a genuine guessing question plus a pinned source comment.** Comments add signal, and sources add
    trust for myth content. Avoid "comment YES" bait.
    https://transparency.meta.com/features/approach-to-ranking/types-of-content-we-demote/
14. **Do collabs with Turkish science, history and geography creators** (up to 3 via Buffer, not with Trial Reels).
    https://emplifi.io/resources/instagram-collaborations-boost-organic-engagement/ ·
    https://buffer.com/changelog/music-collaborators-and-more-for-instagram
15. **Cross-post watermark-free to Facebook, Threads, YouTube Shorts and TikTok**, and remake winners with a
    new hook instead of re-uploading them. Optionally test Instagram library music via Buffer on a Creator
    account. https://www.kontentino.com/q-and-a/common-questions-about-instagram-threads-facebook ·
    https://buffer.com/changelog/music-collaborators-and-more-for-instagram
